Getting Started

To make calls through this CRM, you need SIP trunk credentials from a VoIP provider. Here's what you need:

  • Server IP/Host: The SIP server address provided by your provider
  • Port: Usually 5060 for UDP connections
  • Username: Your SIP account username (often your phone number)
  • Password: Your SIP account password
  • Caller ID: The number shown to people you call
